# Today-I-Learned
A space of concise small things I learn day to day on my journey as an Android Engineer

Lately, through reflection and reading numerous books, I’ve realized that writing forces you to think twice. It challenges you to ask yourself: Have I truly learned this? Do I genuinely understand what I’m writing about?

Also a bit of inspiration from [Josh Branchaud](https://github.com/jbranchaud) & [Wajahat Karim](https://github.com/wajahatkarim3/Today-I-Learned)

This is a space where I document the small things I learn each day to test my understanding, reinforce what I’ve learned, and build a consistent learning system for myself.
